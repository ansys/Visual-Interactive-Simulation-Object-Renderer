## Manual check (server mode)

**Setup.** Open the server-mode page with a multi-part dataset. Keep `visor.log` (under `settings.default_log_dir`;
DEBUG is the default level) and the browser console open. Start with nothing selected and the selection mode on
"Select Part".

**Steps**
1. Click a part.
   - Pass: exactly one new `[trigger] set_part_selected arrived: node_id=<n> selected=True.` line, and the part
     highlights in the tree and in the frame.
2. Click empty space.
   - Pass: exactly one new `[trigger] set_part_selected arrived: node_id=<n> selected=False.` line, and the highlight
     clears.
3. Choose "Select Vertex" in the bottom-middle selection-mode popup, then click a part.
   - Pass: the console shows `[VISOR] geometry pick failed` followed by a `GeometryPickingUnavailableError`, and no new
     `set_part_selected` line appears.
4. On a HiDPI display (devicePixelRatio > 1), with "Select Part" again, click near the right and top edges of a part.
   - Pass: that part is the one selected.

**Fail if any of these happen**
- Two `set_part_selected` lines for one click.
- A different part is selected than the one clicked.
- No line at all.
- `[VISOR] click dropped: no frame drawn yet` appears after a frame is visible.
- `[VISOR] pick_part_at failed` appears.
